Our Roots & Craft

Bringing Sri Lanka's Richest Lime Traditions to Your Table

DG PRODUCTS was founded in Mahiyanganaya with a simple passion: preserving the authentic heritage of traditional Ceylon Lunu Dehi (Salted Lime Pickle). We hand-select freshly harvested, thin-skinned citrus limes grown in lush tropical groves.

Every batch is prepared with ancient sun-curing wisdom, steeped in pure solar salt, chili flakes, and aromatic spices until every lime segment attains an intensely rich, tangy, and digestive flavor profile that elevates any Sri Lankan meal.

Natural Solar Salt Aging

Slow cured under direct sunlight to unlock authentic medicinal goodness and depth.

No Preservatives

Crafted strictly with Lime, Salt, Sugar, and Chili flakes without synthetic colors or binders.
